Contact Calabasas Water Damage Pros

Call any hour. The line is for active leaks, flooding, wet walls and ceilings, and mold after a water problem.

The fastest way to reach us

Calling is the only way to start a job. There is no web form to wait on, because water damage gets worse by the hour and a person on the line can tell you what to shut off while the crew is arranged.

Our office address is shown below with a map. Crews are dispatched from the Calabasas area to homes in West Hills, Topanga, Westlake Village, Woodland Hills, Agoura Hills and Calabasas itself.

What to have ready when you call

  • The address where the water is, and the cross street if you are in a canyon or hillside neighborhood.
  • Where the water is coming from, if you know: a pipe, an appliance, the roof, the toilet or the ground.
  • Whether the water looks clean, cloudy or dark, since that changes how it is handled.
  • Which rooms and floors are wet, and whether any ceilings are sagging.
  • Whether the main water valve is off, and whether you have already turned off power to the wet area (only if you could do it safely).
  • Your insurer's name, if you have it, so we can talk about photos and documentation.

If you smell gas, leave the house first and call SoCalGas at 1-800-427-2200 from outside, and call 911 if anyone is in danger. If a water main outside your home has failed, the Las Virgenes Municipal Water District has an emergency line at (818) 251-2100.
