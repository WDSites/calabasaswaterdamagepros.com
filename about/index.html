About Calabasas Water Damage Pros

A 24/7 water damage and mold line for Calabasas homeowners, with plain answers about how a call works.

What we do

Calabasas Water Damage Pros answers calls about water damage and mold in Calabasas, CA. Neighbors get the same line: West Hills, Topanga, Westlake Village, Woodland Hills and Agoura Hills. The line is open around the clock because a burst supply line at 2 a.m. does not wait for office hours.

When you call, you describe what you see. The dispatcher asks where the water is coming from, whether it is clean, gray or contaminated, and how much of the home is affected. That conversation decides which service fits: emergency water removal for standing water, water mitigation to stop damage from spreading, or mold remediation when moisture has already led to growth.

How a call works

We send licensed and insured restoration professionals to the job. Before work starts, the scope and the price are explained to you, so you know what will be removed, what will be dried and what will be rebuilt later. Same-day response is offered when crews are available, and we do not promise an arrival time because traffic on US-101 and weather in the Santa Monica Mountains change from hour to hour.

Calls are answered by dispatchers who ask a short set of questions about the water source, the rooms affected and any electrical risk, then explain the plan before anyone is sent to your door.

  1. You call

    Describe the source, the rooms involved and anything electrical that is wet.

  2. Scope is explained

    You hear what the crew plans to remove, dry or treat, and what it is likely to involve.

  3. The crew works

    Water is extracted, wet materials are removed or dried, and moisture readings are taken.

  4. Documentation

    Photos and readings are kept so your insurer can see what happened and what was done.

What we do not do

We do not decide insurance claims, and we do not promise that a claim will be approved. Your policy and your insurer decide that, and the California Department of Insurance publishes guides on the claim process for homeowners.

We also do not diagnose health problems. If mold or water exposure is a health worry for someone in your home, that question belongs with a physician. And we are not plumbers: a crew can find and dry what a leak damaged, but a failed pipe, valve or fixture still needs a licensed plumber to repair it.

Why Calabasas homes need local knowledge

Calabasas was incorporated in 1991. According to the City's 2021 Housing Element, 73 percent of its homes predate 1989, and the single biggest share, 37 percent, went up during the 1980s. Homes of that age have supply lines, water heaters and roof flashing that are reaching the end of their service lives, which is why slow leaks inside walls are common calls.

The wet season matters too. The nearby Woodland Hills station has a NOAA 1991 to 2020 annual normal of 16.41 inches. Nearly all of it lands between December and March, and every month from June to September stays under a tenth of an inch. Most of our storm-related calls follow that calendar.

The City of Calabasas Safety Element describes a FEMA flood zone beside Las Virgenes Creek on the west side of town, while most of the city lies outside any designated zone. Look up your own address on the FEMA map before assuming either way.

Standards we point homeowners to

Two published industry standards guide this trade. ANSI/IICRC S500 covers professional water damage restoration, and ANSI/IICRC S520 covers professional mold remediation. We encourage you to ask any company you are thinking of hiring, including us, whether its crews work to those standards and to put the scope in writing.

Under Contractors State License Board rules, a contractor needs a license once the labor and materials on a job reach $1,000. It is worth checking a license on the CSLB site before you sign anything.

Frequently asked questions

Is Calabasas Water Damage Pros an insurance company?

No. We take emergency calls and arrange restoration work. Your insurer decides coverage under your policy, and we do not promise any claim outcome.

Do you repair the broken pipe or appliance too?

Restoration crews extract water, dry the structure and rebuild what was damaged. Fixing the failed pipe, valve or fixture is plumbing work, so you will need a licensed plumber for that part.

Who answers the phone at night?

The same dispatch line is answered around the clock, including nights, weekends and holidays. Tell the dispatcher what you see and what you have already shut off.

Will I know the cost before work starts?

Yes. You hear the scope and the price first. If opening a wall shows more damage than expected, that gets discussed before any extra work is done.

Need help now or just have a question?

The line is answered 24/7. Tell us what you see and we will explain the next step.
