Water Damage Services in Calabasas, CA

Water losses move through stages, and each stage has its own crew, tools and goal. Find the service that matches where your home is right now.

Six services, matched to six situations

A wet house is not one problem. It is a sequence: stop the water, get it out, remove what cannot be saved, dry the structure, rebuild, and sometimes deal with mold that grew while the first steps were delayed. Calabasas Water Damage Pros dispatches licensed and insured contractors for each stage, and the cards below explain what each one actually covers.

Water Mitigation

This is damage control for roughly the first 24 to 72 hours. The crew finds the wet areas with moisture meters, extracts what is standing, and sets air movers and dehumidifiers so drywall, framing and subfloor stop absorbing. It is the service that keeps a small loss from becoming a gutted room.

Water mitigation details →

Water Damage Cleanup

Cleanup is about taking things out and making what remains safe to live around. Soaked carpet pad, wet insulation and swollen baseboard are removed, surfaces are sanitized, and odors are treated. It is the right fit when the water was gray or dirty, or when the damage has already sat for days.

Water damage cleanup details →

Water Damage Repair

Repair begins once the structure has tested dry. Carpenters replace drywall and insulation, install baseboard and flooring, and paint so the repaired area blends with the rest. It is a construction phase, not an emergency one, and it can often be scheduled.

Water damage repair details →

Flood Damage Restoration

Floodwater from storm runoff, a creek or groundwater carries soil and bacteria, so it is treated differently from a broken supply line. The work includes heavier protective gear, more removal of porous materials and extra attention to garages and lower levels.

Flood damage restoration details →

Emergency Water Removal

When the problem is inches of water on the floor right now, this is the single-purpose service: pumps and extractors pull it out fast. Drying and cleanup usually follow, but removal comes first because every hour of contact makes the damage worse.

Emergency water removal details →

Mold Remediation

Mold remediation applies when growth is visible or testing has confirmed it. Crews contain the area, run HEPA air filtration, remove contaminated material under ANSI/IICRC S520 practice, and address the moisture source. It is secondary to water work and never a promise that mold will not return.

How the services work together

On a typical burst-pipe job the order is removal, mitigation, cleanup, then repair. Removal and mitigation happen in the first day or two, cleanup overlaps with them, and repair waits for dry readings. Treating them as separate phases helps you read an estimate. A line item for drying equipment is mitigation. A line item for new baseboard is repair.

Be wary of anyone who wants to start rebuilding before the structure has been measured dry. Painting over wet drywall hides the problem and invites mold. Ask which readings the crew uses to decide a wall is finished, and ask whether they work to ANSI/IICRC S500, the published water damage standard.

What none of these services do is repair the failed pipe, decide your insurance claim or diagnose health issues. A plumber handles the pipe, your insurer decides coverage, and a physician answers health questions. We scope and price the restoration work before it starts.

Costs and insurance across the services

Price depends on water category, the area affected, the materials involved, how long they were wet, and how many days of drying equipment the structure needs. We do not publish numbers because they would be guesses. Call the 24/7 line and describe the loss for a real figure.

Insurance in general terms

Sudden and accidental damage is often covered, while gradual leaks and neglect often are not. Flood is usually excluded from a standard homeowners policy and needs separate flood coverage. Check your policy and ask your insurer, and keep photos and receipts for every protective step you take.

Frequently asked questions

Can I book just one of these services?

Yes. If you only need repair after a different company dried the house, or only need mold removal, that is a valid starting point. Tell the dispatcher what has already been done so the scope matches.

Which service do I need first after a pipe bursts?

Shut off the main valve, then call for water removal and mitigation. Those stop the spread. Cleanup and repair come after the structure is controlled and dry.

Do all six services run 24/7?

The emergency line runs 24/7 and takes calls for any of them. Urgent work such as extraction and mitigation is dispatched around the clock. Repair and mold work are usually scheduled.
